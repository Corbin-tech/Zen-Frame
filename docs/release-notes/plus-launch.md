## New: Zen Frame Plus task helpers

Three AI helpers for the moments a task feels too big, too messy or too hard to size up. Every suggestion is an editable preview, and nothing changes in your planner until you choose **Accept**.

- **Breakdown** turns one task into a checklist of small steps. Pick how detailed: 1–3 steps, 3–4, about 5, or 7 or more. Edit, reorder or delete steps before saving, then tick them off ("2 of 5 steps"). Zen Mode shows your next unchecked step. You can also promote steps into their own cluster.
- **Brain dump** turns a messy paragraph into clusters and tasks, each with a time estimate. Rename clusters, move tasks between them (keyboard or mouse), untick anything to skip, and add to a cluster you already have. Open it from Quick Add or the new Dashboard widget. Your draft stays in this tab only and never goes into backups.
- **Estimate** suggests how long a task will take, with a range ("About 45 min (35–80)").

## Free for everyone: estimates and cluster totals

- Add an estimate to any task with the **+ Estimate** chip: 5, 15, 25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, or a custom time.
- Clusters show the total for open tasks and how many aren't estimated yet ("About 1 hr 10 min · 1 not estimated"), in the task list and on the board.
- When you schedule a task, the end time fills in from its estimate. Auto-plan sizes each block to match.

## Plans

- **Try it free:** sign in with your email and get 20 free helper requests. No card needed.
- **Plus:** $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year (USD, plus any tax), with up to 1,000 helper requests a month.
- Sign in, subscribe and cancel from **Settings → Zen Frame Plus**. Cancelling keeps Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

## Privacy

- The helpers are off until you turn them on in Settings.
- Plus sends only the text you give a helper to Zen Frame's server and our AI provider, Anthropic. Zen Frame doesn't save or log it. [[OWNER: after zero data retention is confirmed, replace with "Nothing is stored."]]
- The rest of your planner still stays in your browser.

## Accessibility

The helpers work with a keyboard and screen reader, respect reduced motion, and show a working state with a Cancel button. [[OWNER: keep this line only once the open accessibility follow-ups in `docs/roadmap/ws8-audit.md` are fixed and the launch accessibility pass is done.]]

## Known limits

- The helpers need an internet connection; the rest of Zen Frame doesn't.
- AI suggestions can be wrong. Review them before you accept.
